## ENG 201 Course Website
{:.no_toc}

This course website was developed in response to having generative AI (GenAI) forced into course learning management systems (LMS) like Classes, where it fabricates information about your progress, steals your writing (and increases false positives in plagiarism detection, putting you at risk of accusations of academic integrity violations), and manipulates people into believing they can outsource _any_ part of their thought process without proportionally atrophying their brains.

This website was also designed to streamline our technological platforms. It serves as your OER textbook for the course, while Discord serves as the forum for all participation activities and low-stakes writing. Classes will be reserved only for submitting drafts of your qualitative research project and sharing any scores with you.
