# Finalize Vlog Selection

This week, you'll finalize your selection of 3 vlogs &mdash; ideally recorded lectures about your research focus by scholars in your discipline &mdash; and begin transcribing and coding them as though they're interview transcripts. 

Your analysis of the connotations of speakers' exact word choices &mdash; i.e. what they're saying and also what they're implying or suggesting through the language they use &mdash; will help clarify for your reader why and how members of your discipline are experiencing and responding to the problem that's impacting your field.

As previously stated, for our purposes in ENG 201, *vlogs* refers to medium-length or long (15-20 min) videos that can substitute for an interview. A vlog that could substitute for a live interview should:

- Be about 15-20 minutes
- Ideally involve an established scholar in your field discussing at least one aspect of your research hypotheses
- Include minimal bias in terms of publication venue, i.e. is published in an open-access online forum that isn't itself inherently known for bias (like YouTube, Vevo, DailyMotion, or a university hosting the lecture, as opposed to a for-profit agency) 

Treat the source as though it's a live interview: transcribe the audio and perform qualitative coding on the resulting transcript, using the strategies you've learned about conducting interviews and coding interview data.
