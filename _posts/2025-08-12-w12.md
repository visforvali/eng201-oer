# Choosing a Vlog (If Needed)

As previously discussed, vlogs (video blogs) in the context of our qualitative research project refers to shortform or medium-length video resources that can substitute for an interview. A vlog that could substitute for a live interview should:

- Be about 15-30 minutes
- Involve an established member of your field speaking to at least one aspect of the issue you're exploring in your project
- Ideally involve an established member of your field speaking to issues that address questions you might have scripted for an interview protocol
- Include minimal bias in terms of publication venue, i.e. is published in an online forum that isn't itself inherently known for bias

Treat the source as though it's a live interview: transcribe the audio and perform qualitative coding on the resulting transcript, using the strategies you're learning about conducting and coding interview data.
